## 1. Core Mechanics & Traversal Hacks

> [!tip] **The Slot 6 Open Rule**
> Quetzal event scripts evaluate **your active party count**. If you carry **5 Pokémon**, all 5 must meet the condition (e.g. all 5 knowing Psychic Terrain or Toxic). This leaves **Slot 6 open** so newly caught legendaries land directly into your active party without going to the PC!

> [!info] **On-Demand Move Relearner**
> Quetzal includes a built-in Move Relearner inside every Pokémon's **Summary Screen -> Moves**. You do **not** need Move Tutors, TMs, or Heart Scales to teach required moves like *Psychic Terrain, Toxic, Rest, Scald, Wish, or Moonlight*.

> [!info] **Player Avatar Swapping**
> Change player skin at any time in `Options -> Player -> Avatar` to fulfill avatar requirements:
> * **Lucas / Dawn / Cynthia:** Required for Sinnoh Creation Trio (Dialga, Palkia, Giratina).
> * **N:** Required for Unova Tao Trio (Reshiram, Zekrom).
> * **Calem / Serena:** Required for Kalos Aura Trio (Xerneas, Yveltal, Zygarde).
> * **Victor / Gloria:** Required for Galar Box Trio (Zacian, Zamazenta, Eternatus).
> * **Florian / Juliana:** Required for Paldean Treasures of Ruin (Wo-Chien, Chien-Pao, Ting-Lu, Chi-Yu).
> * **Aerodactyl / Flying Pokémon:** Fly directly over cracked floor tiles in Sky Pillar and Mirage Tower.

> [!info] **Battle Frontier Island Tickets (100,000 Poké Dollars each)**
> Purchase at the PokéMart directly adjacent to the Battle Frontier Pokémon Center:
> * `Old Sea Map` -> Faraway Island (Mew, Cosmog)
> * `Aurora Ticket` -> Birth Island (Deoxys, Necrozma)
> * `Mystic Ticket` -> Navel Rock (Lugia, Ho-Oh)
> * `Eon Ticket` -> Southern Island (Latios, Latias)


> [!caution] **STRICT RESTRICTION: Level 100 & Battle Box Pokémon Immunity**
> **NEVER teach utility moves** (Psychic Terrain, Toxic, Rest, etc.) to any **Level 100 Pokémon** or any Pokémon in your main battle box (e.g. *Gardevoir, Blaziken, Goodra, Baxcalibur, Hydrapple, Togekiss, Breloom, Linoone, Nidorino*). Their movesets must remain completely untouched! Always use low-level utility Pokémon from PC (e.g. Box 10, Box 13).

> [!tip] **Legendary Re-Catching & Living Dex Rule**
> In Quetzal, you can freely use your caught legendaries to trigger new ones and freely evolve/breed them (*Cosmog -> Cosmoem -> Solgaleo/Lunala, Kubfu -> Urshifu, Manaphy -> Phione*) because Altering Cave and overworld respawns allow unlimited re-captures to maintain a 100% full Living Pokédex!
